The journey

From root to ritual

01
The Harvest
Hand-picked at peak ripeness by farmers we know by name. Only red cherries. No strip picking, ever.
02
Processing
Washed, natural, or honey — the method chosen to honour each variety's natural character and the farmer's tradition.
03
The Crossing
Green beans travel directly from cooperative to our Joburg roastery. No middlemen. Full traceability on every bag.
04
The Roast
Small-batch drum roasting in weekly cycles. Every origin gets its own profile, developed over dozens of test batches.
05
Your Cup
Shipped within 72 hours of roast date. The ritual is yours — we just make sure the ingredient is extraordinary.
