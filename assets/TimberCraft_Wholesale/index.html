Technical Data

Wood Species Specifications

Density, hardness ratings, and grain characteristics for engineering and architectural specifying.

American White Oak (Quercus alba)

Hardwood

A dense, heavy hardwood with a coarse open grain texture and exceptional natural rot resistance. Widely specified for luxury hardwood flooring, cabinetry, and cooperage.

Density: 755 kg/m³
Janka Hardness: 1,360 lbf
Durability Class: Class 2 (15-25 yrs)

American Black Walnut (Juglans nigra)

Hardwood

Prized globally for its rich chocolate hues, intricate grain figure, and outstanding dimensional stability. The gold standard for executive furniture and architectural paneling.

Density: 610 kg/m³
Janka Hardness: 1,010 lbf
Durability Class: Class 3 (10-15 yrs)

Western Red Cedar (Thuja plicata)

Softwood

Naturally resistant to decay, moisture, and insect damage due to organic thujaplicin extractives. Highly stable and ideal for exterior rainscreen cladding and saunas.

Density: 370 kg/m³
Janka Hardness: 350 lbf
Durability Class: Class 2 (15-25 yrs)

Burmese Teak (Tectona grandis)

Exotic Hardwood

Rich in natural silica and essential oils, making it imperious to salt water weathering. Premier timber choice for luxury yacht decking and outdoor architectural millwork.

Density: 665 kg/m³
Janka Hardness: 1,150 lbf
Durability Class: Class 1 (25+ yrs)
