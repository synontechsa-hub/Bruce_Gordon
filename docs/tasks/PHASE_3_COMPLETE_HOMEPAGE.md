# Phase 3 - Complete Homepage

## Goal

Carry the approved BGrafX visual language through a complete, production-quality homepage before Bruce provides detailed visual feedback.

## Scope

- Complete the experience, process, commercial approach, contact and footer narrative.
- Replace prototype-only messaging and links.
- Add factual homepage metadata and structured data.
- Add robots and sitemap routes while retaining `noindex` until release approval.
- Verify responsive behaviour, contact paths, accessibility and production output.

## Content boundaries

- No invented clients, testimonials, awards or performance claims.
- Concept work remains explicitly labelled.
- Commercial language describes engagement models without publishing unapproved prices.
- Deployment, analytics, legacy removal and supporting-page rollout remain out of scope.

## Acceptance

- The homepage reads as one complete editorial experience at all target widths.
- WhatsApp and email actions work and are clearly presented.
- Lint, typecheck and production build pass.
- The local result is ready for Bruce's complete visual critique.
